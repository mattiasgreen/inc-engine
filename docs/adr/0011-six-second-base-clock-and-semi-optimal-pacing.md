# Six-Second Base Clock, Shimmer Overdrive, and Semi-Optimal Pacing

To eliminate early-game button franticness while preserving engaging momentum and mathematically calibrated multi-hour progression, the engine establishes a 6-second global base clock, an active Overdrive shimmer, and dual verification against both 2-hour optimal and 4-hour semi-optimal playthroughs.

1. **Six-Second Global Base Clock**: `GameConfig.clock.baseCycleDurationMs` is set to 6000ms (1 tick every 6 seconds) with Generator 1 producing 6 currency per tick. This anchors early gameplay in a deliberate, rhythmically satisfying cadence where the player's first purchase occurs after 12 seconds (2 ticks) without 84-second dead-starts or frantic 1-second cycling.
2. **Shimmer Overdrive and Calm Progress Bars**: When a generator's effective period drops to 1 Hz or faster (`effectivePeriodMs <= 1000ms`), rather than clamping the progress bar to a motionless static rectangle, the bar displays a continuous scrolling CSS gradient shimmer (`@keyframes overdriveShimmer`) with a `FLOWING` indicator. For generators cycling faster than 15 seconds, live percentage numbers are suppressed to eliminate text flicker, allowing the smooth visual bar to communicate progress cleanly.
3. **Dual Pacing Verification (2h Optimal vs 4h Semi-Optimal)**: The simulator test suite validates both an idealized robot (`OptimalRoiPolicy`) reaching the first Prestige threshold (`1e6`) in ~1.8 to 2.0 hours (~110 minutes), and a realistic human player (`SemiOptimalPolicy`, featuring a 45-second reaction hesitation, occasional generator bias, and three 45-minute offline breaks) reaching first Prestige in ~3.5 to 4.5 hours.
